
## 2026-02-01: Shift to Brutalist-Matte
- **Context:** The previous "Liquid Glass" style relied heavily on blur and glow effects, which increased visual noise and was less consistent with high-end audiophile equipment.
- **Decision:** Officially adopted the "2026 Brutalist-Matte" design language for the Sermon music player.
- **Rationale:** Matte surfaces and crisp hairlines provide better legibility and a more professional, "instrument-like" feel.
- **Constraint:** Progress bars, waveform highlights, and selection states are now strictly grayscale to preserve the calm aesthetic, leaving the accent color only for primary actions and focus.
